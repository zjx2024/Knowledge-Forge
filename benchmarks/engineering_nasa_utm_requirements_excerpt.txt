1 Overview

The UTM TCL 2 software implements the software requirements described in this document. It is a cloud-ready research platform, not an operational system. The platform supports beyond-visual-line-of-sight operations and tracking operations.

Service map

| Strategic Deconfliction Service | checks flight intent | SR-01 |
| USS | submits operation intent | SR-02 |

Requirements

SR-01 The Strategic Deconfliction Service shall check a flight intent before approval.
SR-02 The USS shall submit an operation intent to the service.

The service map links a service to a requirement; the service name and the requirement identifier are not the same entity.
